What we do

Practical electrical work for owners who aren't always here. Every job ends with a written report and photos you can send to your insurer.

Fuse box replacement

Old 80s/90s boards upgraded to current regulations. Faster trips, real RCD protection, and paperwork your buyer's lawyer will love.

Safety check & report

Full electrical inspection with a photo report for your insurer, gestor or future buyer. Fixed price, no surprises.

Second-home monitoring

Smart plugs, leak sensors and alexa/google-home control so you can see from the UK if the RCD tripped — and we go check for you.

EV charger installation

Type 2 chargers for villas and garages, installed to Spanish regulation. Winter-ready for your rented car.

UK-style sockets & earthing

Switched sockets, kettles, earthing checks for British appliances. Same voltage, but we make it feel like home.

24/7 emergencies
